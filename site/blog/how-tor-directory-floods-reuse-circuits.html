Version scope: Tor 0.4.9.14 with our custom metrics patch. Tor 0.4.9.15, released October 28, adds directory-request DoS controls, including per-circuit BEGIN_DIR rate limiting and a hidden-service directory (HSDir) request limit. The measurements below describe 0.4.9.14, not the effect of those new controls.

Relay labels, such as Relay O, are anonymized. BEGIN_DIR requests a directory stream. Positions count BEGIN_DIR cells on each circuit; position 2+ means the circuit already carried one.

Cell positions. A circuit can carry separate streams. The BEGIN_DIR command asks to open a directory stream; the counter alone does not prove a successful opening. Positions count BEGIN_DIR cells only. Closing a stream does not reset the circuit’s position, and a first-position cell does not establish when its circuit was created.

Methods #

Populations. A majority is more than half the whole period’s own population. The longer group combines positions 101–1,000 and 1,001+. Eleven relays appear in both longer and shorter groups, in different observations; 10 periods meet neither group’s threshold. Cell positions and stream classifications use separate counters, so individual cells cannot be paired with stream outcomes. These are aggregate associations, not causal effects.

Categories. “Closed while waiting for an HTTP request” is our custom metric label for directory streams that close in Tor’s HTTP-request-wait state, with an empty input buffer and no earlier request classification. It does not identify the cause. Other classifications combines the remaining six categories: Consensus GET requests, Microdescriptor GET requests, Server / extra-info descriptor GET requests, Other GET requests, Other POST requests and Unparsed directory streams. Coverage and timing. The frozen observations span October 24–29, 2026. The 44 periods with minute-rate gaps retain their cumulative counts. Public starts and durations are rounded to 15-minute blocks; their sum gives the displayed end. Average observation spans use original durations before rounding and are not necessarily complete floods or continuous behavior.

HSDir circuit use. Its first/repeat counts combine GET and POST. Client-channel and closing/unavailable-circuit rejections are counted separately before reuse counting. These populations are separate from BEGIN_DIR positions and do not establish client delivery.
